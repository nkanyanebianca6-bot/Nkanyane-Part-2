About Masingitas Travel and Tours

Company Background & History

Masingitas Travel and Tours was initially founded in 2008 by visionary entrepreneur Khanyisa Masingita Ngobeni with a passion for sharing South Africa's scenic beauty with the world. Following a strategic pause in operations, the business officially relaunched in 2018 with renewed digital infrastructure, expanded commercial partnerships, and a focus on delivering affordable, high-quality travel management to both domestic and international clientele.

Headquartered in South Africa, Masingitas Travel and Tours has grown into a fully recognized, VAT-registered travel agency holding professional accreditations from international aviation bodies and local tourism organizations. Our focus centers on making tourism accessible, convenient, and memorable while fostering growth within Southern Africa's economy.

Our Travel Experience

Our Mission, Vision, and Core Values

Our Mission Statement

To provide highly convenient, affordable, and customized travel solutions for individuals, families, corporate organizations, and educational institutions, ensuring that every journey—whether local or international—is executed smoothly and safely.

Our Vision Statement

To grow into a premier, globally trusted digital travel management company recognized across Southern Africa for excellence in customer care, reliable service delivery, and innovative digital booking systems.

Core Business Values

Our operations are guided by four essential pillars:

  • Integrity: Conducting business with complete financial transparency, honesty, and accountability.
  • Customer Centricity: Tailoring every travel itinerary around the unique preferences and financial budgets of our clients.
  • Reliability: Partnering exclusively with verified, top-tier accommodation providers, airlines, and transport operators.
  • Excellence: Continuously improving our service delivery to exceed customer expectations at every phase of travel.

Who We Proudly Serve

We serve a wide and diverse range of travelers across various industries and demographics:

  • Families & Leisure Groups: Vacation planning, holiday resort bookings, and group getaway packages.
  • Corporate & Government Officials: Streamlined corporate travel arrangements, executive shuttle transfers, and conference venue bookings.
  • Couples & Honeymooners: Romantic, private weekend escapes and luxury lodge getaways.
  • School & Academic Groups: Structured, safe educational tours, museum visits, and cultural heritage field trips.
  • International Tourists: Inbound travel coordination, game safari arrangements, and guided city tours across South Africa.

Travel for Everyone

Industry Accreditations & Compliance

Masingitas Travel and Tours proudly maintains full compliance with key international and local travel authorities:

  • IATA Accredited: Certified by the International Air Transport Association to issue international and domestic air tickets directly.
  • ASATA Member: Proud member of the Association of South African Travel Agents, upholding strict professional standards of business conduct.
  • VAT Registered: Operating in full accordance with South African commercial and tax regulations.
